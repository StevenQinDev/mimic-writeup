# Event and Challenge Links / 赛事与题目入口

This page collects the Rare Byte Hub event pages and per-challenge listings. The bilingual writeups in this repository are independent; the links are provided for the original prompts, event information, and the platform's article listings.

Rare Byte Hub 的赛事页面和逐题列表集中列在这里。本仓库的中英文题解是独立作品；这些链接用于查看原题、赛事信息和平台提供的文章列表。

Organizer pages may require an event account or be unavailable after the competition. Link availability does not affect the archived writeups or the local reproduction checks.

组织方页面可能需要赛事账号，或在赛后暂时不可用。链接是否在线不影响仓库中的题解和本地复现检查。

The published [event rules](https://ctf.rarebytehub.com/events/mimic/rules) allow writeups after the event. They do not state a separate license for redistributing challenge prompts or attachments; those materials remain attributed and excluded from the repository's MIT license.

公开的[赛事规则](https://ctf.rarebytehub.com/events/mimic/rules)允许赛后发布 writeup，但没有单独授予题面或附件的转载许可；这些材料保留署名，并排除在仓库 MIT 许可之外。
