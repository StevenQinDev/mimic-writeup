## Original statement

````text
January 2022. Polar night.

A satellite ground station at the top of the world loses one of its two undersea fiber links to the mainland. Days earlier, a fishing trawler had been zigzagging over the exact stretch of seabed where the cable failed.

We have a photo of the station.

Find the station, the trawler, and the trawler's IMO number and radio call sign.

Flag format: RBH{STATION_IMO_CALLSIGN}
(Station name is 7 letters.)
````
