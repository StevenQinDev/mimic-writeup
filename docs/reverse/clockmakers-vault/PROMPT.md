````text
Deep in the Swiss Alps, an abandoned clockmaker's workshop
was recently reopened.

Inside lies a steel vault with no lock, only a small screen
asking for a passphrase. Legend says the clockmaker locked
a tiny machine inside its mechanism.

The passphrase is 32 characters long.
Open the vault and the flag is yours.
````
