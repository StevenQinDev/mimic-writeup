## Original statement

````text
The river does not erase. It only stops pointing.

Mnemosyne, keeper of everything ever remembered, chose to forget exactly one thing. She left no record of what, and none of why.

You wake with three memories you cannot place.

Find the last thing she remembered.

→ Enter the river
````
