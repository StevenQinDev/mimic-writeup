# About These Writeups / 关于本文集

## Purpose and scope / 目的与范围

This collection documents all 19 Mimic CTF 2026 challenges. The articles follow the path from clue to model to verification and preserve the calculations, scripts, and public sources needed to check the result.

本文集记录 Mimic CTF 2026 的 19 道题。文章按照“观察线索 → 建立模型 → 验证结果”的顺序组织，重点保留能够重新检查的计算、脚本和公开来源。

## AI assistance / AI 使用

AI tools assisted with parts of the analysis, initial drafts, translation, and helper scripts. Executable code, captured output, and source links are included so the technical claims can be checked directly.

部分分析、初始文稿、翻译和辅助脚本由 AI 工具参与完成。仓库保留可运行代码、实际输出和来源链接，读者可以依此检查技术结论。

## Official solution references / 官方题解参考

Every article is compared with the organizer’s published solution, and all 38 articles include a direct link. The [source manifest](solution-sources.json) records the references: 18 official articles print matching flags; the Telegram article corroborates only its workflow. Lethe’s echo-discovery chain, Postmarked’s request observations, and the visual discovery of Halvard’s ship name are explained with attribution to those sources. The prose is independently written, and reproducible alternative methods are included where they provide a clearer or independent check.

每篇文章都对照了组织方公开题解，38 篇文章均提供直达入口。[来源清单](solution-sources.json) 记录对照来源：18 篇官方文展示了相同 flag，Telegram 只确认流程。Lethe 的回声发现链、Postmarked 的请求观察，以及 Halvard 船名的视觉发现均明确参考官方资料。正文独立撰写；在能够提高清晰度或提供独立校验时，也保留其他可复现的解法。
